In March 2024, Northwind Robotics acquired Helix Analytics for $42 million to accelerate its computer-vision roadmap. 
After the acquisition, Helix's founder, Priya Raman, joined Northwind as VP of Product Analytics and now reports to CTO Daniel Li. 
Northwind operates R&D labs in Austin and Leipzig, while Helix's data ingestion pipelines continue running from the Dublin region in Google Cloud. 
Although the combined company announced a “single customer data model,” finance later clarified that billing still flows through the legacy Helix tenant until Q4 2025. 
During the migration, the observability team replaced Prometheus with OpenTelemetry exporters; however, several alerts were disabled by mistake, which caused a three-hour outage on April 17, 2024.
Following the postmortem, SRE lead Maria Santos mandated canary rollouts for all ingestion services and assigned remediation tasks to squads Atlas and Borealis.
Separately, enterprise customer Orion Health expanded its contract on July 2, 2025, adding a HIPAA Business Associate Agreement; as a result, PII must be stored exclusively in the us-central1 region. 
Meanwhile, the Berlin sales team continues to collaborate with the Austin lab on a pilot for anomaly detection in radiology images, using a model fine-tuned from Llama-3.1 with 12 million de-identified scans. 
Despite early false positives, precision improved after Dr. Ahmed refactored the labeling guidelines and introduced a two-stage review workflow.
